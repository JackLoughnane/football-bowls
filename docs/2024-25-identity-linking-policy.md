# 2024/25 identity linking policy

The official handbook extract has 702 player-club records, including 574 with appearances. An exact normalized name shared across clubs is **only a candidate identity link**. There are 17 such groups (34 rows) in the 2024/25 staging extract.

Do not use a name, surname, or equal appearance count as a permanent player ID. In particular, abbreviated names like Neto, Ward, Becker and Vardy need external identification. A verified cross-club link requires a stable external player ID or two independently documented identifiers (e.g. full name, date of birth, transfer history) with an evidence URL. Record reviewer and date.

The staging file is **not approved for import**. Club codes identify clubs, not players. The game's required measure is career Premier League appearances *for a specific club*, summed from verified player-club-season rows. Zero-appearance handbook records must not become eligible answers.

Before import, audit unique player identifiers, club identity, transferred player links, name collisions, season-specific appearances, duplicate rows, and provenance. Do not sum appearances across different clubs for the same player's club-specific total.
